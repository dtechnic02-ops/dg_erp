# PART 1 — Accounting Core Foundation

## 1. Mission

The mission of DG ERP Accounting Core is to provide one unified, immutable, auditable, company-isolated accounting engine for every financial transaction inside DG ERP.

Every financial transaction generated anywhere in the ERP must eventually become one official accounting entry.

No module may maintain its own accounting system.

---

## 2. Purpose

- Maintain complete financial integrity.
- Produce official accounting records.
- Generate legally auditable financial reports.
- Protect accounting history.
- Eliminate duplicate accounting logic.
- Ensure every module follows identical accounting rules.
- Preserve permanent accounting history.
- Guarantee balanced accounting entries.

---

## 3. Scope

### Phase 1

- Chart of Accounts
- Accounting Entry
- Accounting Entry Line
- General Ledger
- Trial Balance
- Posting Engine
- Financial Year Integration
- Business Date Integration
- Opening Balance
- Operational Account Mapping
- Sub Ledger
- Reversal
- Security
- Accounting Reports

### Excluded From Phase 1

- Journal
- Journal Entry
- Profit & Loss
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow
- Budget
- Cost Center
- Multi Currency
- Consolidation
- Fixed Asset Depreciation
- Bank Reconciliation

---

## 4. Accounting Philosophy

1. Every financial event creates accounting.
2. Accounting never depends upon UI.
3. Accounting belongs to the business.
4. Accounting records are permanent.
5. Delete is forbidden; correction occurs through reversal.
6. Every accounting entry must balance.
7. Every accounting entry belongs to exactly one company.
8. Every accounting entry belongs to exactly one Financial Year.
9. Every accounting entry uses Business Date.
10. Accounting reports are generated only from official accounting tables.

---

## 5. Official Accounting Architecture

Official accounting tables:

- chart_accounts
- accounting_entries
- accounting_entry_lines

Operational tables are not accounting books.

---

## 6. Source of Truth

Accounting Core is the only source of truth for:

- General Ledger
- Trial Balance
- Future Journal
- Profit & Loss
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow

---

## 7. Accounting Ownership

Only the centralized Accounting Posting Service may create official accounting entries.

Business modules must never directly insert, update, or delete accounting tables.

---

## 8. Immutability

Posted accounting entries cannot be edited or deleted.

Corrections occur only through approved reversal while preserving the original history.

---

# PART 2 — Chart of Accounts Constitution

## Chapter 1 — Purpose

### 2.1 Purpose

The Chart of Accounts (COA) is the permanent financial classification system of DG ERP.

Its purpose is to provide a standardized, company-isolated, and auditable structure for recording every accounting transaction generated by the ERP.

The Chart of Accounts defines where financial information is stored. It does not store business transactions. It only defines the financial destination of accounting entries.

### 2.2 Objectives

- Provide one standardized accounting structure for all companies.
- Support Double Entry Accounting.
- Support General Ledger.
- Support Trial Balance.
- Support future Financial Statements.
- Eliminate duplicate account structures.
- Maintain long-term accounting consistency.
- Preserve accounting history.
- Prevent unauthorized accounting modifications.

### 2.3 Authority

Every accounting entry created anywhere inside DG ERP shall reference a valid Chart Account.

No accounting entry may exist without a Chart Account.

Applicable modules:

- Sales
- Purchase
- Purchase Return
- Sales Return
- Income
- Expense
- Loan
- Opening Balance
- Inventory
- Journal
- Future Accounting Modules

---

## Chapter 2 — Account Classes

DG ERP supports exactly five root account classes.

| Class | Normal Balance | Statement |
|------|------|------|
| Asset | Debit | Balance Sheet |
| Liability | Credit | Balance Sheet |
| Equity | Credit | Balance Sheet |
| Income | Credit | Profit & Loss |
| Expense | Debit | Profit & Loss |

These classes are permanent and cannot be renamed or deleted.

---

## Chapter 3 — Account Hierarchy

Maximum hierarchy:

Level 1 → Root Class

Level 2 → Group / Control Category

Level 3 → Posting Account

Example:

Asset

└── Current Asset

    └── Cash in Hand

Rules:

- Maximum depth is Level 3.
- One parent only.
- Same company.
- Same account class.
- No circular references.
- No self-parent.
- No cross-company hierarchy.

Only Level 3 accounts may receive accounting postings.

---

## Chapter 4 — Account Types

### System Account

- Created by ERP.
- Mandatory.
- Protected.
- Cannot be deleted.
- Used by Accounting Posting Engine.

### Control Account

Used for sub-ledger integration.

Examples:

- Accounts Receivable
- Accounts Payable
- Inventory
- Loan Payable

### User Account

Created by Company Admin.

Examples:

- Rent Expense
- Electricity Expense
- Vehicle Maintenance

---

## Chapter 5 — Company Isolation

Every Chart Account belongs to one company only.

Prohibited:

- Shared accounts
- Cross-company posting
- Cross-company hierarchy
- Cross-company references

---

## Chapter 6 — Account Code Standard

Rules:

- Unique Account Code within each company.
- Reserved System Codes cannot be reused.
- System Code cannot be modified.
- Account Code cannot change after postings exist.

---

## Chapter 7 — System Account Constitution

### 7.1 Purpose

System Accounts are permanent accounting accounts automatically created and managed by the DG ERP Accounting Engine.

These accounts are essential for maintaining accounting integrity and ensuring consistent financial reporting across all companies.

System Accounts shall exist before any business transaction is performed.

---

### 7.2 Definition

A System Account is an account that is owned by the ERP system rather than by the company user.

The ERP uses these accounts automatically during accounting postings.

System Accounts are protected and cannot be treated as ordinary user-created accounts.

---

### 7.3 Ownership

System Accounts are owned by the Accounting Core.

Company users may use them through business transactions but shall not own them.

The Accounting Engine remains the final authority over every System Account.

---

### 7.4 Creation Rules

System Accounts shall be created automatically during company initialization.

Manual creation of mandatory System Accounts is prohibited.

If a required System Account does not exist, company activation shall not be completed.

---

### 7.5 Mandatory System Accounts

Every company shall contain, at minimum, the following System Accounts:

### Assets

- Cash in Hand
- Bank Account
- Accounts Receivable
- Inventory

### Liabilities

- Accounts Payable
- Loan Payable
- Tax Payable

### Equity

- Capital
- Retained Earnings

### Income

- Sales Revenue
- Other Income

### Expense

- Purchase
- Operating Expense

Additional mandatory accounts may be introduced in future versions of DG ERP.

---

### 7.6 System Code

Every System Account shall contain a permanent System Code.

Examples:

```
SYS_CASH
SYS_BANK
SYS_AR
SYS_AP
SYS_INVENTORY
SYS_CAPITAL
SYS_RETAINED_EARNINGS
SYS_SALES
SYS_PURCHASE
```

System Codes shall be globally reserved.

Duplicate System Codes are prohibited.

---

### 7.7 Modification Rules

The following fields shall never be editable:

- System Code
- Account Type
- Root Class

The following may be configurable where permitted:

- Display Name
- Description

Business logic shall determine which properties are editable.

---

### 7.8 Delete Policy

System Accounts shall never be deleted.

Soft Delete is prohibited.

Hard Delete is prohibited.

If a System Account is no longer required by a future version, it shall be deprecated instead of deleted.

---

### 7.9 Posting Rules

The Accounting Posting Engine may automatically post into System Accounts.

Business modules shall never directly choose a protected System Account unless the Business Constitution explicitly allows it.

---

### 7.10 Security

Only the Accounting Engine may create or manage System Accounts.

No Company Staff, Company Admin, Super Staff, or Super Admin shall manually create, delete, or change protected System Accounts.

---

### 7.11 Audit

Every change to a configurable property of a System Account shall be permanently logged.

The audit log shall include:

- Company
- Account
- Old Value
- New Value
- User
- Date
- Time
- Reason

Audit history shall never be removed.

---

### 7.12 Validation Rules

The system shall reject:

- Duplicate System Codes.
- Missing mandatory System Accounts.
- Posting to inactive System Accounts.
- Changing Root Class.
- Cross-company references.
- Manual deletion.
- Manual recreation using reserved System Codes.

---

### 7.13 Future Compatibility

Future modules including:

- Sales
- Purchase
- Expense
- Income
- Loan
- Journal
- Inventory
- Payroll
- Fixed Assets

shall use these System Accounts through the Accounting Posting Engine.

No future module may redefine the meaning of a System Account.

---

## Chapter 8 — Chart Account CRUD Constitution

### 8.1 Purpose

This chapter defines the constitutional rules governing the creation, modification, activation, deactivation, locking, and deletion of Chart Accounts.

These rules ensure that accounting integrity is maintained throughout the lifetime of every account.

---

### 8.2 Create Rule

A new Chart Account may be created only if all mandatory validations are satisfied.

Mandatory requirements:

- Company must be active.
- Financial Year must exist.
- Parent Account must be valid.
- Account Class must be selected.
- Account Code must be unique.
- Account Name must be unique within the same parent where applicable.
- User must have Create permission.

The system shall reject duplicate Account Codes.

---

### 8.3 Edit Rule

An account may be edited only when permitted by this Constitution.

Editable fields may include:

- Account Name
- Description
- Display Order
- Remarks

The following fields shall never be editable after creation:

- Company
- Account Class
- System Code
- Root Account
- Parent Hierarchy (if postings already exist)

---

### 8.4 Posting Protection Rule

If an account has one or more posted accounting transactions:

The following operations are prohibited:

- Changing Account Class
- Changing Normal Balance
- Moving to another Root Class
- Replacing System Code
- Recreating the account

Accounting history shall remain permanently valid.

---

### 8.5 Delete Rule

Chart Accounts shall never be physically deleted after creation.

Hard Delete is prohibited.

Soft Delete is prohibited for:

- System Accounts
- Control Accounts
- Accounts with posting history

Only unused User Accounts without references may be permanently removed before first use, subject to Business Owner policy.

---

### 8.6 Deactivate Rule

Instead of deletion, User Accounts may be marked as Inactive.

Inactive accounts:

- Shall remain visible in historical reports.
- Shall not accept new postings.
- Shall preserve all accounting history.

---

### 8.7 Restore Rule

Inactive User Accounts may be reactivated.

Reactivation shall not alter:

- Account Code
- Posting History
- Account Class
- Audit History

---

### 8.8 Lock Rule

The system may automatically lock accounts under the following conditions:

- Financial Year Closed
- Account Frozen by Business Owner
- System Protection Enabled
- Company Locked

Locked accounts shall reject all modification requests.

---

### 8.9 Rename Rule

Changing the display name of a User Account is permitted when business policy allows.

Renaming shall never modify:

- Account Code
- System Code
- Historical Transactions
- Audit Records

Historical reports shall remain consistent after renaming.

---

### 8.10 Validation Rules

The system shall reject:

- Duplicate Account Codes
- Duplicate System Codes
- Circular Parent References
- Posting to Inactive Accounts
- Editing Locked Accounts
- Deleting Protected Accounts
- Invalid Root Class Changes

---

### 8.11 Audit Requirements

Every CRUD operation shall permanently record:

- Company
- Account
- Action Type
- Previous Value
- New Value
- User
- Date
- Time
- IP Address (where available)
- Reason

Audit records shall never be deleted.

---

### 8.12 Constitutional Principles

Chart Accounts are permanent accounting objects.

Business requirements may evolve.

Accounting history shall not.

Every CRUD operation shall prioritize accounting integrity over operational convenience.

---

## Chapter 9 — Account Lifecycle Management Constitution

### 9.1 Purpose

This chapter defines the complete lifecycle of every Chart Account from its creation until its permanent retirement.

Every Chart Account shall follow one standardized lifecycle governed by the Accounting Core.

No module or developer may bypass these lifecycle rules.

---

### 9.2 Lifecycle Stages

Every Chart Account shall pass through the following stages:

1. Creation
2. Validation
3. Activation
4. Business Usage
5. Posting
6. Locking
7. Deactivation
8. Reactivation (if permitted)
9. Retirement

The lifecycle shall be permanent and fully auditable.

---

### 9.3 Account Creation

Before creating an account the system shall verify:

- Company exists
- Company is active
- Financial Year exists
- Parent account is valid
- Account Class is valid
- Account Code is unique
- Account Name is valid
- User has permission

Creation shall fail if any validation fails.

---

### 9.4 Opening Balance

Opening Balance may be assigned only during approved Opening Balance procedures.

Opening Balance shall:

- belong to one Financial Year
- belong to one Company
- create official Accounting Entries
- update General Ledger
- become permanently auditable

Opening Balance shall never bypass the Accounting Posting Engine.

---

### 9.5 Operational Usage

Business modules shall never post directly into accounting tables.

Modules shall submit accounting requests through the Accounting Posting Service.

Examples:

Sales

↓

Posting Service

↓

Accounting Entry

↓

Ledger

↓

Trial Balance

The business module shall never calculate ledger balances.

---

### 9.6 Sub Ledger Integration

Control Accounts may connect to Sub Ledgers including:

- Customer
- Supplier
- Employee
- Loan
- Bank
- Inventory

Sub Ledgers shall never replace the General Ledger.

General Ledger remains the official accounting record.

---

### 9.7 Modification Rules

The following may be changed where permitted:

- Display Name
- Description
- Display Order

The following shall never change:

- Company
- Account Code
- System Code
- Account Class
- Historical Posting

---

### 9.8 Locking Rules

Accounts shall automatically become locked when:

- Financial Year is Closed
- Company is Locked
- Business Owner freezes the account
- System Protection is enabled

Locked accounts reject every modification.

---

### 9.9 Deactivation

Inactive accounts:

- remain in reports
- preserve posting history
- reject future postings

Historical accounting shall never disappear.

---

### 9.10 Retirement

Retired accounts remain permanently stored.

Retirement does not remove:

- Accounting Entries
- Ledger
- Audit History
- Trial Balance History

Retirement affects future usage only.

---

### 9.11 Audit

Every lifecycle event shall permanently record:

- Company
- Account
- Lifecycle Event
- User
- Date
- Time
- Reason

Audit history shall never be deleted.

---

### 9.12 Constitutional Principles

Every Chart Account has one permanent lifecycle.

Business may change.

Accounting history shall not.

The Accounting Core shall always prioritize financial integrity over operational convenience.

---

# PART 3 — General Ledger Constitution

## Chapter 1 — Purpose

The General Ledger (GL) is the official accounting book of DG ERP.

Every posted accounting transaction shall ultimately be reflected in the General Ledger.

The General Ledger is the only official source for account balances.

---

## Chapter 2 — Objectives

- Maintain complete account history.
- Record every debit and credit movement.
- Support Trial Balance generation.
- Support Financial Statements.
- Preserve permanent accounting records.
- Ensure full auditability.

---

## Chapter 3 — Ledger Principles

1. Every ledger entry originates from an official Accounting Entry.
2. Manual ledger insertion is prohibited.
3. Ledger records are immutable after posting.
4. Every ledger entry belongs to one company.
5. Every ledger entry belongs to one Financial Year.
6. Every ledger entry uses the Business Date.

---

## Chapter 4 — Ledger Posting Rules

Each ledger transaction shall contain:

- Company
- Financial Year
- Business Date
- Account
- Debit Amount
- Credit Amount
- Running Balance
- Source Module
- Source Document
- Reference Number

---

## Chapter 5 — Running Balance

The system shall automatically calculate the running balance after every posted transaction.

Running balances shall never be edited manually.

Balance calculation depends on the account's normal balance (Debit or Credit).

---

## Chapter 6 — Restrictions

The following actions are prohibited:

- Delete ledger records.
- Edit posted ledger records.
- Duplicate ledger records.
- Cross-company postings.
- Cross-financial-year postings.

Corrections shall occur only through approved reversal entries.

---

## Chapter 7 — Audit Rules

Every ledger transaction shall maintain:

- Creation history
- Posting reference
- Source module
- Source document
- User responsible for posting
- Date and time of posting

Audit history shall be permanent.

---

## Chapter 8 — Integration

The General Ledger provides data for:

- Trial Balance
- Profit & Loss
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow
- Accounting Reports

No report may calculate balances directly from operational tables.

---

## Chapter 9 — General Ledger Lifecycle Constitution

### 9.1 Purpose

This chapter defines the complete lifecycle of every General Ledger transaction from its creation until its permanent archival.

The General Ledger is the official accounting book of DG ERP.

Every posted accounting transaction shall permanently exist within the General Ledger.

---

### 9.2 Ledger Creation

General Ledger records shall be created automatically by the Accounting Posting Service.

Manual ledger creation is prohibited.

Every ledger transaction shall originate from a valid Accounting Entry.

---

### 9.3 Ledger Validation

Before creating a General Ledger record, the system shall verify:

- Company
- Financial Year
- Business Date
- Accounting Entry
- Debit Amount
- Credit Amount
- Chart Account
- Posting Status

Creation shall fail if any validation fails.

---

### 9.4 Running Balance

Running Balance shall be calculated automatically.

Users shall never edit running balances.

Running Balance shall always be reproducible from Accounting Entries.

---

### 9.5 Ledger Modification

Posted ledger records shall never be modified.

The following actions are prohibited:

- Edit
- Delete
- Replace
- Merge
- Split

Corrections shall occur only through approved reversal.

---

### 9.6 Ledger Lock

Ledger records shall automatically become locked when:

- Financial Year is Closed
- Company is Locked
- Audit Lock is enabled

Locked records shall reject every modification attempt.

---

### 9.7 Ledger Archival

Historical ledger records shall remain permanently available.

Archiving shall never change:

- Debit
- Credit
- Balance
- Posting Reference
- Audit History

---

### 9.8 Ledger Audit

Every ledger transaction shall permanently record:

- Company
- Financial Year
- Business Date
- Source Module
- Source Document
- Posting Reference
- User
- Date
- Time

Audit history shall never be deleted.

---

### 9.9 Constitutional Principles

The General Ledger is immutable.

Every financial movement shall exist exactly once.

The General Ledger remains the permanent accounting history of the company.

---

# PART 4 — Trial Balance Constitution

## Chapter 1 — Purpose

The Trial Balance is the official financial verification report of DG ERP.

Its purpose is to verify that the total Debit balance equals the total Credit balance for a company and Financial Year based exclusively on posted accounting records.

---

## Chapter 2 — Objectives

- Verify accounting accuracy.
- Confirm Debit equals Credit.
- Detect accounting imbalance.
- Serve as the foundation for financial statements.
- Support internal and external audits.

---

## Chapter 3 — Source of Data

The Trial Balance shall be generated only from the official General Ledger.

Operational modules such as Sales, Purchase, Expense, Income, Inventory, Cash and Bank shall never be used directly to calculate Trial Balance figures.

---

## Chapter 4 — Generation Rules

A Trial Balance shall always be generated using:

- Company
- Financial Year
- Business Date / Date Range
- Posted Transactions Only
- Active Chart of Accounts

Draft, cancelled or unposted accounting entries shall be excluded.

---

## Chapter 5 — Balance Rules

For every account the report shall display:

- Account Code
- Account Name
- Debit Balance
- Credit Balance
- Net Balance

The report must satisfy:

Total Debit = Total Credit

If the totals differ, the Trial Balance is invalid.

---

## Chapter 6 — Restrictions

The Trial Balance is a report only.

Users shall not:

- Edit balances.
- Insert balances.
- Delete balances.
- Override calculated values.

All values must be generated automatically from accounting records.

---

## Chapter 7 — Audit Rules

Every Trial Balance shall be reproducible from the same accounting data.

The report shall preserve:

- Company
- Financial Year
- Date Range
- Generation Time
- User
- Report Parameters

---

## Chapter 8 — Integration

The Trial Balance is the official source for:

- Profit & Loss
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow
- Accounting Reports
- External Audit

No financial statement shall bypass the Trial Balance process.

---

## Chapter 9 — Trial Balance Governance Constitution

### 9.1 Purpose

This chapter defines the governance, validation, generation, verification, publication, and preservation rules of the DG ERP Trial Balance.

The Trial Balance is the official financial verification report generated from the General Ledger.

---

### 9.2 Report Authority

The Trial Balance shall be generated only by the Accounting Core.

No business module shall calculate or modify Trial Balance values.

The Trial Balance shall always represent official accounting data.

---

### 9.3 Report Generation

The Trial Balance shall always be generated using:

- Company
- Financial Year
- Business Date or Date Range
- Posted Transactions Only
- Active Chart of Accounts

Draft, cancelled, deleted or reversed transactions shall follow the Accounting Constitution.

---

### 9.4 Validation Rules

Before generating the report, the system shall verify:

- Company exists
- Financial Year is valid
- Business Date is valid
- Ledger is balanced
- Accounting Entries are complete
- Required accounts exist

Generation shall stop if any validation fails.

---

### 9.5 Balance Verification

The Trial Balance shall satisfy:

Total Debit = Total Credit

If Debit and Credit totals are not equal, the report shall be marked as invalid.

No financial statement shall be generated from an invalid Trial Balance.

---

### 9.6 Report Security

Users shall never:

- Edit balances
- Insert balances
- Delete balances
- Override calculated values

All balances shall be read-only.

---

### 9.7 Audit Requirements

Every generated Trial Balance shall permanently record:

- Company
- Financial Year
- Report Period
- Generated By
- Generation Date
- Generation Time
- Report Parameters

Audit history shall never be deleted.

---

### 9.8 Historical Integrity

Historical Trial Balances shall remain reproducible.

Re-generating the same report using identical accounting data shall produce identical results.

Historical financial reports shall never change without an approved accounting reversal.

---

### 9.9 Report Integration

The Trial Balance is the official source for:

- Profit & Loss
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow
- Financial Reports
- External Audit
- Government Reporting

No report shall bypass the Trial Balance.

---

### 9.10 Constitutional Principles

The Trial Balance is the official verification report of DG ERP.

Every financial statement shall originate from a valid Trial Balance.

Accounting accuracy shall always take precedence over reporting convenience.

---

# PART 5 — Accounting Engine, Security & Developer Constitution

## Chapter 1 — Accounting Posting Engine

The Accounting Posting Engine is the only authorized mechanism for creating official accounting entries.

All financial modules shall submit accounting impacts through the Posting Engine.

Direct database insertion into accounting tables is prohibited.

---

## Chapter 2 — Posting Workflow

Official posting sequence:

1. Business Transaction Approved
2. Validation Completed
3. Financial Year Verified
4. Business Date Verified
5. Accounting Entry Created
6. Accounting Entry Lines Created
7. General Ledger Updated
8. Trial Balance Updated
9. Accounting Reports Available

If any step fails, the entire posting process shall be rolled back.

---

## Chapter 3 — Validation Rules

Before posting, the system shall verify:

- Company
- Financial Year
- Business Date
- Active Account
- Debit = Credit
- User Permission
- Posting Status
- Duplicate Reference Prevention

Posting shall be rejected if any validation fails.

---

## Chapter 4 — Reversal Constitution

Posted accounting entries shall never be edited or deleted.

Corrections shall occur only through reversal.

A reversal shall:

- Preserve the original entry.
- Create an equal and opposite accounting entry.
- Maintain a complete audit trail.
- Record reversal reason and author.

---

## Chapter 5 — Security Constitution

Only authorized users may post accounting entries.

Users shall not:

- Delete posted accounting.
- Modify posted accounting.
- Bypass validations.
- Change Financial Year history.

Every accounting action shall be permission-controlled.

---

## Chapter 6 — Audit Constitution

Every accounting event shall permanently record:

- Company
- Financial Year
- Business Date
- Source Module
- Source Document
- Reference Number
- Posting User
- Posting Timestamp
- Reversal Information (if applicable)

Audit history shall never be deleted.

---

## Chapter 7 — Performance Rules

The Accounting Engine shall:

- Support large transaction volumes.
- Prevent duplicate posting.
- Maintain transactional consistency.
- Use database transactions for atomic operations.
- Scale for multi-company SaaS environments.

---

## Chapter 8 — Developer Constitution

Developers shall not:

- Insert directly into accounting tables.
- Update posted accounting records.
- Delete accounting history.
- Calculate ledger balances manually.
- Duplicate accounting logic inside business modules.

Developers shall always use the official Accounting Posting Service.

---

## Chapter 9 — Future Compatibility

The Accounting Core shall remain compatible with future modules including:

- Journal
- Profit & Loss
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow
- Budget
- Cost Center
- Multi Currency
- Consolidation
- Fixed Assets
- Bank Reconciliation

---

## Chapter 10 — Accounting Engine Governance Constitution

### 10.1 Purpose

This chapter establishes the governance rules for the DG ERP Accounting Engine.

The Accounting Engine is the central financial processing component responsible for converting approved business transactions into official accounting records.

Every accounting transaction shall be processed according to this Constitution.

---

### 10.2 Engine Authority

The Accounting Engine is the only component authorized to:

- Create Accounting Entries
- Create Accounting Entry Lines
- Update the General Ledger
- Update the Trial Balance
- Maintain Posting History
- Execute Accounting Reversal

No other service, module, API or developer code shall perform these operations directly.

---

### 10.3 Processing Sequence

Every accounting process shall execute in the following order:

1. Business Validation
2. Permission Validation
3. Financial Year Validation
4. Business Date Validation
5. Account Validation
6. Duplicate Validation
7. Accounting Entry Creation
8. Accounting Entry Line Creation
9. General Ledger Update
10. Trial Balance Update
11. Audit Log Creation
12. Transaction Commit

If any step fails, the complete transaction shall be rolled back.

---

### 10.4 Transaction Integrity

The Accounting Engine shall execute every posting inside one database transaction.

Partial posting is prohibited.

Incomplete accounting records shall never exist.

---

### 10.5 Duplicate Prevention

The Accounting Engine shall prevent duplicate posting by verifying:

- Source Module
- Source Document
- Company
- Financial Year
- Posting Reference

Duplicate accounting records shall be rejected automatically.

---

### 10.6 Error Handling

When an error occurs:

- No Accounting Entry shall remain.
- No Ledger record shall remain.
- No Trial Balance impact shall remain.

The complete posting process shall be cancelled.

System errors shall be permanently logged.

---

### 10.7 Reversal Processing

The Accounting Engine shall process reversals using official reversal procedures only.

Reversal shall:

- Preserve original posting.
- Create opposite accounting entries.
- Update the General Ledger.
- Update the Trial Balance.
- Preserve complete audit history.

---

### 10.8 Performance

The Accounting Engine shall:

- Support concurrent posting.
- Support high transaction volume.
- Prevent deadlocks.
- Ensure ACID database transactions.
- Scale for Multi-Company SaaS environments.

---

### 10.9 Security

The Accounting Engine shall reject:

- Unauthorized posting.
- Direct database modification.
